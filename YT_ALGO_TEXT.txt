01 — WHAT IS THE ALGORITHM? CORE PHILOSOPHY
The YouTube algorithm is an AI-powered recommendation system designed to match each individual
viewer with the most relevant video from the 500+ hours of content uploaded every minute. The algorithm
has one fundamental objective: keep viewers on the platform by helping them discover content they enjoy,
and ensure they return repeatedly.
KEY CONCEPT: Pull Architecture
YouTube does NOT push your videos to an audience. Instead, viewers' interests PULL content toward
them. Your job is to create content worth pulling. This means the algorithm does not find an audience for
your video — it finds the right video for each viewer.
As of 2026, YouTube processes over 80 billion signals per day to answer one question: 'Will this specific
viewer enjoy this specific video right now?' The algorithm now evaluates channel-level patterns and
long-term viewer satisfaction more heavily than individual video performance.
The biggest paradigm shift of 2025: YouTube moved from evaluating individual videos in isolation to
evaluating channels as a whole. Instead of rewarding a single viral video, the algorithm now favors
channels with a consistent identity, repeatable formats, and a predictable upload schedule. These
channels are significantly easier to recommend.

02 — 5 INDEPENDENT RECOMMENDATION SYSTEMS
YouTube is not a single algorithm. It consists of five separate recommendation systems, each operating
under different rules with different objectives and metrics.
HOME (Home Feed)
The personalized feed viewers see when they open YouTube. It is based on watch history, recent
interactions, and preferences of users with similar profiles. Small channels can appear on the Home feed if
they have strong packaging (thumbnail + title) and high retention. Subscriber count is NOT a factor.
Primary Signals: CTR + Video Velocity (momentum in the first few hours after upload)
SUGGESTED (Suggested Videos)
The recommendations displayed alongside or below the video currently being watched. The algorithm
aims to continue the viewing session by predicting what the current viewer will want to watch next. Linking
your own videos together (via playlists, end screens) is critical here.
Primary Signals: Topic similarity + Viewer profile matching + Session continuation
SEARCH (Search Results)
Results that appear when a viewer types a query. Keyword relevance is the initial filter, followed by ranking
based on satisfaction signals. Two different users searching the same query may see different results
because their personal watch histories differ. For type beat channels, Search is the MOST IMPORTANT
surface.
Primary Signals: Keyword relevance + Personal watch history + Retention
SUBSCRIPTIONS (Subscriptions Feed)
The feed showing new uploads from subscribed channels. Inactive subscribers lower your CTR. Organic,
gradual growth is healthier than a large but passive subscriber base.
Primary Signals: Upload consistency + Subscriber activity level
SHORTS (Shorts Feed)
The vertical, swipeable feed. Operates entirely differently from long-form content. In 2026, YouTube added
Shorts-specific search filters, making SEO relevant for Shorts as well. Shorts can serve as a discovery tool
that drives traffic to long-form videos.
Primary Signals: Swipe-away rate + Completion rate + Loop rate + Search relevance

03 — 3 SIGNAL CATEGORIES THE ALGORITHM
MEASURES
In 2026, YouTube's recommendation system processes hundreds of signals, but all of them fall into three
core categories. Understanding these three groups is the foundation of all strategy.
A) CLICK SIGNALS
Impressions (number of times your thumbnail is shown), CTR (percentage of impressions that result in
clicks), and your video's performance relative to other options shown to the same viewer. Important: High
CTR combined with low retention is harmful because YouTube interprets this as a broken promise (the
thumbnail/title set an expectation the video failed to deliver).
 Target CTR: 4-10% depending on niche
 CTR below 3%: Thumbnail or title is not compelling enough
 90% of top-performing videos use custom thumbnails
 Strong facial expressions in thumbnails can increase CTR by 20-30%
B) WATCH / RETENTION SIGNALS
Average view duration, audience retention rate, and session duration. 50-60% average view duration is
considered solid. 70% or higher earns priority placement in Suggested videos. Low retention is usually
solved by improving the intro, not by making shorter videos — the most common problem is a slow first 30
seconds.
 Retention matters more than raw watch time: A short video watched to completion beats a long video
with 40% retention
 Use pattern interrupts (visual change, music shift, or rhythm change) every 20-30 seconds
 In the first 7-10 seconds, clearly state what the viewer will gain from watching
C) SATISFACTION SIGNALS (2025-2026 Game Changer)
This is the biggest change in 2025-2026. YouTube now collects millions of post-watch survey responses
asking viewers whether they were satisfied with what they watched. Machine learning models trained on
these survey responses now predict satisfaction for ALL users.
 Repeat views: Does the viewer re-watch the video? (Strong positive signal)
 Survey responses: Did the video meet the viewer's expectations?
 'Not Interested' clicks: Are people actively avoiding the content? (Strong negative signal)
 Sentiment analysis: Comment tone and sentiment now influence recommendations
 Post-session behavior: Does the viewer continue using YouTube after watching, or leave the
platform?

04 — 4-TIER DISTRIBUTION MODEL
When a video is uploaded, YouTube does NOT show it to everyone simultaneously. It tests the video in
progressive tiers, measuring performance at each stage. Videos that perform well are promoted to the next
tier.
TIER 1: Core Audience
Subscribers and regular viewers. The video is first shown to a small group already familiar with your
channel. CTR and retention are measured. If strong signals are received, the video advances to the next
tier. If not, the video stalls at this tier.
TIER 2: Recent Viewers
People who have watched your content recently but may not be subscribed. They share similar interest
profiles. Engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) are evaluated. Positive signals continue the
expansion.
TIER 3: Topic Match
People who watch content in your topic area but do NOT know your channel. Clear packaging (thumbnail
+ title) wins at this tier. These viewers are interested in your topic but unfamiliar with your brand. Correct
packaging + relevance = discovery.
TIER 4: Adjacent Audiences
People who watch related but different topics. This is the tier where videos go viral. It reaches viewers in
adjacent interest areas. Reaching this tier requires strong performance in all three previous tiers.
IMPLICATION FOR TYPE BEAT CHANNELS: In your early uploads, you must build a consistent and
clearly defined niche to feed Tiers 1 and 2. For ENNY TANG, this means every video must speak the
same visual language (Night Vision LUTs, consistent thumbnail style, uniform title format). The algorithm
must be able to 'recognize' your channel and route it to the correct viewers.

05 — CTR: CLICK-THROUGH RATE STRATEGY
CTR (Click-Through Rate) measures what percentage of people who see your thumbnail actually click and
watch. It is the algorithm's first gatekeeper — if CTR is low, your video cannot reach its potential.
THUMBNAIL BEST PRACTICES
 One strong visual idea: Focus, not clutter
 High contrast: Must be readable at small sizes on mobile screens
 Minimal text: If any, no more than 3-4 words in bold font
 Consistent brand style: A recognizable visual language across all videos
 Mobile-first design: 70%+ of viewers are on mobile devices
TITLE BEST PRACTICES
 Keep titles under 60 characters (prevents truncation on mobile)
 Formula: Keyword + curiosity trigger
 Type beat title format: [FREE] Artist1 x Artist2 Type Beat - "Beat Name"
 Do NOT use misleading titles — high CTR + low retention = algorithmic penalty
CTR OPTIMIZATION PROCESS
If CTR is below 3% within 48 hours of upload, test a new thumbnail and/or title. Use YouTube's Test &
Compare feature to A/B test up to 3 different thumbnails per video. Spend 2 hours on your thumbnail and 2
minutes on tags — NOT the reverse. Tags have minimal impact; 95% of discovery is driven by thumbnail,
title, and retention.

06 — RETENTION: THE ART OF KEEPING VIEWERS
Retention measures how much of your video viewers actually watch. In 2026, efficiency matters more than
length: a short video watched to 100% completion with high satisfaction sends a much stronger signal than
a 20-minute video with only 40% retention.
RETENTION STRATEGY FOR TYPE BEAT VIDEOS
Type beat videos are primarily consumed as music listening content and have unique retention patterns
compared to other content types. In the first few seconds of the beat, lead with the most attention-grabbing
part of the melody or rhythm. Place your producer tag at the 3-5 second mark, not at the very beginning.
The beat's natural structure (intro, verse, chorus, bridge) creates organic pattern interrupts — which is
exactly what YouTube rewards.
THE FIRST 30 SECONDS RULE
Most viewers decide whether to stay or leave within the first 30 seconds. For type beats, this means
placing the catchiest part of the beat (usually the chorus melody) in the first few seconds. Cut long ambient
intros.
WEEKLY METRIC TARGET: Each week, set one specific metric goal. Example: 'Increase first-30-second
retention from 62% to 70%.' Then design the intro of your next two videos specifically to hit that target.

07 — SATISFACTION SIGNALS (2025-2026 Game
Changer)
The most significant part of YouTube's 2025-2026 transformation is the shift from raw metrics to viewer
satisfaction. The platform no longer measures only how much was watched, but whether the viewer felt
their time was well spent.
HOW SATISFACTION IS MEASURED
Post-Watch Surveys
YouTube sends millions of surveys monthly asking 'Was this video worth your time?' The responses are
used to train ML models that predict satisfaction for all users, not just survey respondents.
Repeat Views
If a viewer re-watches a video, it is a strong satisfaction signal. This is especially relevant for type beats:
producers and rappers repeatedly listen to beats they like.
'Not Interested' Clicks
Carries strong negative weight. If viewers are actively avoiding your content, this is a serious penalty
signal.
Comment Sentiment Analysis
The tone and sentiment of comments now directly influence recommendations. Positive comments =
positive signal.
Post-Session Behavior
Does the viewer continue using YouTube after watching your video, or do they leave the platform
entirely?

08 — YOUTUBE SEO: SEARCH ENGINE
OPTIMIZATION
YouTube is part of the world's largest search engine (Google) and is itself the second-largest search
engine. For type beat channels, SEO is the primary discovery method because viewers actively search for
terms like '[artist name] type beat'.
KEYWORD RESEARCH
 YouTube search bar autocomplete: If suggestions appear when you type 'Travis Scott type', search
volume exists for that term
 Google Trends: Track artist popularity trends over time
 Long-tail keywords: Specific queries like 'dark trap type beat 2026' offer lower competition
 The JETBOX SEO engine's 9-tier, 117-artist system is already aligned with this principle
METADATA OPTIMIZATION
Title format: [FREE] Artist1 x Artist2 Type Beat - "Beat Name" | Genre + Year
Description: Place primary keywords in the first 2-3 lines. Include contact information, license types, and
social media links. Target a minimum of 200+ words.
Tags: Have minimal impact but include main artist names and genre labels. Primary focus should remain
on title and description.
Captions/Subtitles: Verify automatic subtitle accuracy — YouTube reads captions to understand content.
2026 SEO UPDATE
YouTube now performs deep content analysis — visuals, audio, and speech. This means the words used
in your title should also be present within the video itself (e.g., spoken or displayed). YouTube has become
significantly better at understanding what a video is 'about' and matching it to relevant interest clusters.

09 — YOUTUBE SHORTS ALGORITHM
The Shorts algorithm operates entirely differently from long-form content. In long-form, viewers choose
what to watch; in Shorts, they discover content by swiping through a feed. Shorts has reached 200 billion
daily views.
SHORTS RANKING FACTORS
 Swipe vs. Watch Rate: The #1 metric. If viewers instantly swipe past, the video fails.
 Completion Rate: Was the video watched to the end?
 Loop Rate: Did the video auto-replay and get re-watched?
 Engagement: Likes, comments, shares, and especially new subscribers gained from the Short
 Search Relevance (NEW in 2026): Shorts-specific search filters were added — title and description
optimization is now critical for Shorts
SHORTS STRATEGY FOR TYPE BEAT CHANNELS
Use Shorts as a discovery tool, NOT as your primary revenue channel. Package the best 15-30 seconds
of a beat as a Short and link to the full-length video in the description. Behind-the-scenes beat-making
clips, hook/melody teasers, and beat-switch moments perform well. In 2026, Shorts expanded to 3
minutes, providing more room for storytelling.
SHORTS → LONG-FORM FUNNEL: Shorts provides discoverability; long-form videos provide deep
engagement and session duration. When this funnel works correctly, Shorts serves as an entry point and
long-form content builds loyal viewers. Shorts monetization remains low ($0.01-0.06 per 1K views); real
revenue comes from long-form.

10 — OPPORTUNITY WINDOW FOR SMALL
CHANNELS
In 2026, small channels have a genuine advantage. The algorithm prioritizes viewer response over
subscriber count or upload history. YouTube introduced the 'Hype' feature for channels with 500 to
500,000 subscribers: fans can Hype a new video to push it into a special ranking panel and earn a
temporary boost in the Explore feed.
EVIDENCE FOR SMALL CHANNELS
 Channels with fewer than 1,000 subscribers account for 30% of the top 100 videos in niche trending
topics
 New channels showing strong CTR and retention in their first videos are tested with broad audiences
within days
 YouTube is actively creating pathways for new channels to find their audience faster
 Hype feature (500-500K subscribers): Fan-driven support to bypass algorithmic barriers within the
first 7 days of upload
MINIMUM VIDEO THRESHOLD: New channels should upload at least 20-30 videos before the algorithm
can reliably recommend them. Uploading 5 videos and concluding 'the algorithm hates me' is incorrect —
the algorithm simply does not KNOW you yet. Consistent publishing trains the subscription algorithm to
keep informing your audience.

CHANNELS
The type beat market exists at a unique intersection of the YouTube algorithm: content is search-heavy
(users actively search for it), retention patterns are specific to music listening, and the target audience

ENNY TANG JETBOX PIPELINE ADVANTAGES
 Consistent Visual Identity: Night Vision LUTs (RAGE preset: green tint, MELODIC preset: pink tint)

Optimize based on data. Which artist names generate higher CTR? Which beat genres show better

Phase 3: Scaling (100+ Videos)
The algorithm now knows your channel well. Tier 3-4 audience reach begins. Evergreen content (older
beats) can resurface. Strengthen satisfaction signals through community engagement (reply to

12 — OPTIMAL UPLOAD TIMES (US MARKET)

your video collects the best possible data from its first test pool. Poor timing can bury content regardless of
quality.
CORE RULE: Upload 2-3 Hours BEFORE Peak Viewing Hours. Uploading at peak time means you are
already late. YouTube's servers need 2-3 hours to process the video (4K rendering, copyright checks,
subtitle generation) and index it. The video should be fully ready for distribution by the time your viewers
are most active.

13 — MYTHS VS. FACTS
MYTH: 'Tags are very important'
FACT: Tags have minimal impact. Thumbnail, title, and retention drive 95% of discovery. Spend 2
hours on your thumbnail and 2 minutes on tags.
MYTH: 'Longer videos always perform better'
FACT: The right length depends on the idea's requirements. A 6-minute complete answer beats a
20-minute padded video. Efficiency > length.
MYTH: 'The algorithm is biased against small channels'
FACT: Small channels are being tested more aggressively than ever. Channels under 1,000
subscribers account for 30% of the top 100 in niche trending topics.
MYTH: 'Never change the title or thumbnail'
FACT: Strategic iteration is healthy. If CTR is below 3% after 48 hours, test a new title/thumbnail.
MYTH: 'Clickbait works'
FACT: It may generate initial clicks, but if viewers leave early, retention drops and the algorithm
detects this. Misleading thumbnails erode trust and visibility over time.
MYTH: 'You must upload every day'
FACT: Quality matters more than quantity. Too much low-quality content lowers viewer
satisfaction — a negative signal for the algorithm. Find a sustainable rhythm.
MYTH: 'A video must gain traction within one day or it is dead'
FACT: YouTube does not stop promoting videos after one day. Videos can gain momentum days,
weeks, or even months later — as long as watch time and engagement continue, they can
resurface.

14 — WEEKLY ACTION PLAN
The following plan provides a repeatable weekly workflow for the ENNY TANG channel's first 90 days.
Repeat this cycle every week.
MONDAY — Production + Analysis
Review the previous week's YouTube Analytics: CTR, retention, traffic sources. Identify which videos
performed well and why. Begin beat production — prepare the first beats of the week.
TUESDAY — Upload Day #1
Process the first beat through the JETBOX pipeline: metadata, artwork, LUT color grading, SEO
generation. Upload at 10:00 PM Turkey time (2:00 PM EST). Monitor engagement for the first 2 hours after
upload.
WEDNESDAY — Community + Shorts
Reply to comments on the previous day's upload. Prepare a short Shorts clip from the beat. Post a
community update.
THURSDAY — Upload Day #2
Upload the second beat. Thursday has high CPC in the US market. Run a thumbnail A/B test.
FRIDAY — Upload Day #3 + Shorts
Upload the third beat (entertainment/gaming audiences are active on Fridays). Create a Short from the
week's best-performing beat.
SATURDAY — Additional Upload + Analysis
Optional 4th beat upload (5:00 PM Turkey time = 9:00 AM EST). Conduct weekly performance
comparison. Plan artist/genre targets for next week.
SUNDAY — Strategy + Rest
Summarize the week's metrics. Decide which artist tiers to target next week. Scan trends. Rest.
